About

This is my personal website, where I practise drawing and slowly get better at building things on the internet.

I post comics when one is finished and pictures when I like them enough. There is no posting schedule and there never will be.

Everything here is drawn by me. The site is a pile of HTML files, a stylesheet and one small script — no framework, no build step, no cookies.

If you want to see something drawn, there's a suggestion box.
